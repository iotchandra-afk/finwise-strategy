From first trusted decision to durable paid value.

03 / Earn the next review04

When “paid” is not “available.”

Decision Delta compares the original plan with a *verified changed fact*—and must distinguish a bookkeeping state from spendable cash.

Invoice A marked paid · funds pending$10,400 gap

The risk remains. A paid accounting flag alone cannot change the bank-available forecast or retire the warning.

Invoice A $12,800 available on day 5$2,400 headroom

Synthetic modeled day-11 low becomes $22,400. The owner reassesses—but only after verifying taxes, holds and cutoffs.

Real retention mechanism: save decision → verified material change → evidence-backed comparison → owner keeps, revises or escalates. When nothing meaningful changes, silence is good product behavior.

04 / Measure what matters05

Instrument the decision.
Then test the mechanism.

Business-account cohorts connect behavioral evidence to commercial outcomes; a notification click or longer session does not.

L1 · Verified first decision7-day activation / all pre-eligible trial businesses

Real usable data, documented assumptions, critical obligation coverage and owner-recorded disposition. Failed imports stay in the denominator.

L2 · Verified second reviewBy day 28 / activated businesses

A material changed fact or deliberate reappraisal and new decision record. Report who had a genuine review opportunity.

G1 · First paid conversionValid payments / trial starts at comparable term

Case baseline 2%. Segment by source and reverse-trial handoff; do not use L1 as proof of commercial improvement.

G2 · Retention + contribution90/365-day paid survival and account margin

Case annual retention 40%. Include source fees, exception handling, support and acquisition economics before declaring growth.

Non-negotiable gate: A false “funds available / all clear,” missed critical obligation, or confidentiality failure is a product incident—not an acceptable conversion trade-off.
Only aggregate case facts are observed. No user-level event data, actual correlations or experimental effects are provided.FINWISE / 05

240–365 days

Scale or change course

Expand only where mature paid survival, contribution and trust improve.

The approval I would seek

Instrumentation, financial-data quality controls, focused owner research and a gated activation experiment—not a blanket paid-acquisition increase or premature price launch.

The outcome that earns expansion

More retained profitable paying businesses per qualified trial, with no material worsening in financial decision safety.
