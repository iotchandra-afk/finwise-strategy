2. Proof before prompting

A production cash improvement requires a bank-reconciled amount available before the actual obligation cutoff. Invoice status, settlement, posting and spendable availability are different states.

  • Decision still belongs to the same authorized business account.
  • A source version must prove usable funds; “marked paid” alone leaves the earlier risk in place.
  • Bank holds, a missing payroll-tax amount, an unknown ACH cutoff or stale balances block an “all clear.”
  • A projected floor breach near a confirmed cutoff is higher urgency than a cosmetic forecast change; only the authorized owner acts.
  • No routine changed-facts alert is sent if cash did not change, even if the user has not logged in.

Privacy-safe notification preview: “Your saved cash plan changed. Review it privately in FinWise.” No customer name, payroll amount, or account balance appears in a push/email subject.
