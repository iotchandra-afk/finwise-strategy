A deliberate trade-off

The fastest path is not always the best path. We introduce the minimum verification needed to avoid turning an untrusted forecast into a confidently wrong decision.

Live-product requirement: permissioned connection or import, readable freshness timestamp, reconciliation status, explainable assumptions, and a correction path.

This prototype has no bank/ledger integration and cannot verify actual balances or receivables.
